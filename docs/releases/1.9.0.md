# AXQuant 1.9.0

1.9 is a **precision-allocator** toolkit release on **Apple / MLX**. It does
not replace 1.8 convert, `optimize`, affine U32 packs, or certificates.
Certified production converts may stay on `axquant[mlx]==1.8.1`.

CUDA, NVFP4, and a portable OCP MX interchange are out of 1.9. They are a
2.x line. `--q-mode mxfp4` remains the MLX Apple packer.

## Added

- `axquant diagnose-joint` enumerates a small `(weight BPW × KV bits × context)`
  grid under one memory budget and optionally computes
  `I(W, KV) = dQ(W, KV) − dQ(W) − dQ(KV)` from a BF16 baseline plus three
  matched treatment quality evaluations. Winners require `--kv-analysis`.
  Target BPWs below the architecture policy minimum are skipped instead of
  aborting the whole grid.
- `axquant plan-joint` turns that grid into a convert-ready development
  plan. Small I keeps 1.8 independent `optimize`. Material I ranks feasible
  cells with `additive + I * u(W) * u(KV)` and writes `axquant_plan.json`.
  Same effective `(weight BPW, per-layer KV)` reuses the independent plan.
- Development schemas `axquant.joint-interaction.v1` and
  `axquant.joint-selection.v1` (`certification_eligible=false`).
- Operator notes: [docs/experimental-joint-interaction.md](../experimental-joint-interaction.md).

## Changed

- Package version is `1.9.0`. Planning, convert, validate, and `verify-cert`
  semantics are the 1.8 path plus the optional joint search.
- Resident-loadable Qwen 3.5/3.6/3.8 MTP sidecars now carry the canonical
  `qwen3-next-mtp` runtime identity used by strict oMLX import and MTPLX
  discovery. The change is metadata-only, preserves existing exactness
  evidence, and keeps the Super-class SSD-stream pack out of the resident
  runtime contract.

## Operator action

- Keep certified production converts on `axquant[mlx]==1.8.1` until you
  choose to promote 1.9.
- Run inspect / convert / diagnose-joint / plan-joint for real models on
  `df-macstudio-m2`, not on a laptop.
- Try `axquant plan-joint --help`. Output cannot authorize a Hub pack or
  certificate.
- Do not cite `joint-interaction.json` as a public quality or speed claim.
- Do not treat a 1.9 MXFP4 pack as an OCP or NVIDIA checkpoint.
